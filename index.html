FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit to Aurah Beauty Parlour.

How long does a typical beauty service take? +
Service duration varies based on the treatment. Haircuts take 30-45 minutes, facials 60-90 minutes, and bridal packages 2-3 hours. Our team will provide timing details during booking so you can plan your visit accordingly.
Do I need to book an appointment in advance? +
While walk-ins are welcome, we highly recommend booking in advance to ensure availability and minimize wait time. You can book easily through our website, WhatsApp, or by calling us directly.
What products do you use for services? +
We use only premium international brands like L'Oréal, MAC, Schwarzkopf, and Moroccanoil. All products are carefully selected for quality and safety to ensure the best results for your hair and skin.
Do you offer bridal makeup packages? +
Yes! We offer comprehensive bridal packages including pre-wedding grooming, trial makeup, and complete bridal look for the big day. Our packages can be customized to your specific needs and budget.
What should I do to prepare for my appointment? +
For hair services, come with clean, dry hair. For facials, arrive makeup-free if possible. For waxing, let your hair grow at least 2-3 weeks. Our team will provide specific pre-care instructions when you book your appointment.
